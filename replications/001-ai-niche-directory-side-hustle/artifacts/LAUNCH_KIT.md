## 1. 1-Hour / $0 Facebook Marketplace & Local Group Validation Post

**Where to Post**: Local Brides/Wedding Planning Facebook Groups, City Subreddits (`r/Austin`, `r/Dallas`, `r/Atlanta`), or Facebook Marketplace Services category.
**Validation Goal**: `>= 5` inbound DMs/comments within 48 hours before purchasing a domain or scaling.

```text
Title: FREE LIST: 12 Austin & Dripping Springs Event Venues That Allow Outside Catering + BYOB ($0 Corkage Fee)

Body:
After calling 40+ local venues and reading their fine-print catering contracts, I put together a verified spreadsheet + searchable directory of local event/wedding venues where:
✅ You can bring ANY licensed outside caterer or cultural/family chef
✅ Food trucks are allowed on-site with hookups
✅ BYOB beer, wine & liquor is permitted with $0 corkage/buyout fee (saves ~$3,800+ on an average 150-guest reception)

Comment "VENUE" or DM me your guest count + target month and I'll send you the top 3 matching venues and their exact outside-catering rules for free.
```

## 2. "Free Lead First -> $30/mo Featured Placement" Vendor Outreach Script

> **Why This Script Works (Off-Camera Trap #1 Fix)**: Never cold-email a business asking for `$30/month` on an empty directory. Wait until your directory or Facebook validation post captures a real consumer inquiry, forward that lead to the business **for free**, and then offer the `$30/mo` Featured Spot on the second follow-up.

### Email 1: Sending the Free Qualified Lead (Day 1)
```text
Subject: New event inquiry for [Venue Name] (180 guests - Outside Catering + BYOB)

Hi [Venue Manager Name],

I run CaterFree Venues, a local directory specifically for couples and corporate planners searching for venues that allow licensed outside caterers and BYOB.

A couple planning a 180-guest reception for [Target Month] just used our filter tool looking for a venue in [Neighborhood/City] with a commercial prep kitchen and open catering policy, and [Venue Name] was one of their top matches.

Here are their details at no charge:
- Name: [Lead Name]
- Email / Phone: [Lead Contact]
- Event Specs: [Guest Count, Date, Catering/BYOB notes]

Feel free to reach out to them directly! Also, please let me know if any of your outside-catering or corkage policies on your listing page need updating: [Link to Listing]

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, CaterFree Venues
```

### Email 2: The $30/mo Featured Placement Upgrade Pitch (Day 5)
```text
Subject: Re: New event inquiry for [Venue Name] + Featured Top Spot

Hi [Venue Manager Name],

Hope the conversation with [Lead Name] went well!

We currently have [X,XXX] monthly local planners filtering for open-catering venues in [City]. We only allow 2 "Verified Featured Partners" pinned to the very top of [City] search results (with instant SMS/email routing on "Get 3 Free Quotes" requests).

It's a flat $30/month (month-to-month, cancel anytime—one booked event covers 10+ years of the listing).

If you'd like to claim one of the two Featured Partner spots for [Venue Name], you can activate it in 60 seconds here:
[Stripe $30/mo Checkout Link]

Best,
[Your Name]
```
